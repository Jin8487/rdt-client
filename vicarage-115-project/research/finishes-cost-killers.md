## The plays (channel evidence, 2026)

1. **John Pye weekly bathroom auctions — Cradley Heath B64 5RE, 8 miles.** Liquidation/retail-return suites, enclosures, vanities hammer at 20–40% of RRP. Viewing Mon–Fri in person. Landed maths: hammer + ~20–25% premium + VAT on premium. Save ~£1.5–2k on bathrooms. johnpyeauctions.co.uk /Browse/C20021808/BATHROOM-AND-PLUMBING
2. **BPI / i-bidder / Britannia trade-lot auctions** — Porcelanosa tile pallets, composite doors, LVT lots, unreserved bathroom liquidations. Cherry-pick branded lots only; buy 15% overage; forklift collection logistics.
3. **B-grade/outlet porcelain**: Minoli Outlet from £5/m² (up to 80% off, lines to 1,000m²); Johnson Tiles Factory Outlet; Tile-Clearance; TileZone. £45/m² Italian porcelain at £12/m² is the same tile. Single-batch — cannot rematch; 15% overage; tell the tiler it's B-grade before agreeing rates.
4. **GF floor verdict: polished-screed-as-finish LOSES.** Grind-and-seal £45–100/m² on top of the slab vs clearance herringbone SPC (~£20–34/m² supply; FloorMonster/Floors4You) + £12–15 fitting. Microcement £85–155/m² is an upcharge. Winning stack: liquid anhydrite screed tendered ~£20/m² (Midland Screed Floor, Liquid Floor Screed West Midlands) + clearance herringbone SPC, wear layer ≥0.5mm.
5. **Kidderminster carpet seconds (30 min away)**: Carpet Factory Outlets — Cormar remnants/end-of-rolls/slight seconds at ~£6–10/m² vs £18–22 retail S+F. Take room sizes, go in person; tone-different colours per room reads "designed". DO NOT paint chipboard upstairs — reads student-house.
6. **Walls: refuse the whole-house skim upsell.** Tape-and-joint Level 4 + quality dead-matt paint everywhere; buy Level 5 only on ~80m² of light-critical surfaces (rooflight-washed walls, stairwell, ceilings under raking light). Skim adds £8–15/m² for nothing on most walls.
7. **V-groove/shaker MDF panelling feature walls** from sheet stock: ~£15/m² materials; three full-height feature walls ≈ £700 all-in vs £2.5–4k quoted. Full-height, wide rails, sprayed — timid narrow strips read cheap.
8. **Roof**: attic trusses stay (cut roof costs MORE in labour); Marley Edgemere-class interlocking slate ~£14–15/m² new (Roof Giant £339.20/pallet of 240); reclaimed/seconds only if ONE matched lot covers 75m²+10% — mixed weathering reads patchy on new work; EPDM beats GRP (£80–120 vs £100–150/m²) and the 28m² centre is invisible.
9. **Rooflights: RoofLITE+/Dakea (Altaterra = VELUX Group's own budget brands)** — 30–40% under Velux, Velux-compatible flashings, 10-yr guarantee. ~£230–280/unit. Keep Velux only where a specific blind/electric opener is wanted.
10. **Windows: Polish landed pack (Fenbro, Debesto)** — reference tilt-turn €246–368; ~half UK price landed; Fenbro Summer 2026 promo. 6–10 week lead — ORDER EARLY; specify trickle vents (Part F); remakes take 8 weeks so pay the fitter to survey.
11. **Doors: paint-grade ply flush doors in bulk (Premdor), sprayed** + oversized square-edge MDF skirting; FD30 flush leaves in the same bulk order. £40–60/door hanging in volume. The ironmongery carries the look — £15–25/set solid levers.
12. **Services**: electrics per-point tender (£80–150 nat., Midlands lower; ~80 points); plumbing price-work, keep boiler, oversized flat-panel rads (heat-pump-ready) — UFH not in bill so "skipping" it saves nothing, BUT the caveat: in-screed UFH pipe laid before the liquid screed pour is the cheapest UFH moment ever (~£1.2–1.8k GF) and a resale wow — consider. MVHR not required: trickle vents + dMEV wet-room fans satisfy Part F (~£250 vs £3.5–5k).
13. **PIR seconds for GF floor build-up** (Seconds & Co, ICB Manchester, Kennings "Kingspan 2nds") — 40–60% off, invisible under screed forever. Keep the datasheet for Part L sign-off.
14. **Loft stairs**: made-to-measure straight softwood flight online (~£800–1,200) + price-work fit; painted stringers, dark runner (Kidderminster), oak-capped handrail. Confirm 42° max pitch / 1.9m headroom / opening size BEFORE ordering.
15. **Decoration: airless spray the empty shell** (2–3 days vs 7–10 brush), then brush feature colours. Spray = the flat hotel finish; it's what makes paint-grade doors work. Sequence before flooring/second fix. Spend £300 of the saving on premium dead-matt.
16. **Kitchen (outside bill)**: DIY Kitchens (verified ~50% under Wren/Howdens quotes, rigid-built) or ex-display Howdens (Used Kitchen Hub, Rehome); stone worktop remnants Birmingham (Hereford Stone, Palazzo) up to 75% off — remnant quartz island + laminate runs is the classic hack. Showroom look £6–9k vs £15–20k quoted.
17. **Brassware strategy**: contract ceramics + premium touchpoints ONLY (tap, valve, flush plate, towel rail, 8mm glass). Never a £12 mixer on £5/m² porcelain — the tap is the tell. Victorian Plumbing clearance (up to 60% off), Wholesale Domestic ex-display.
